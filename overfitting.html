The Overfitting Playground

The amber points are noisy measurements of a smooth curve. Give the model more flexibility and watch what happens to its error on data it has never seen. The training error will keep falling. The test error will not.

What to look for

  • Start at degree 0 and move up. At first both errors fall together, which is underfitting giving way to a real fit.
  • Keep going past the lowest test error. The curve starts to wiggle through single points while the unseen points drift away from it.
  • Lower the training points to 8 and raise the complexity. With nearly as many coefficients as points, the model can hit every point exactly.
  • Raise the noise. The noise floor is the lowest test error any model can reach, because the noise itself cannot be predicted.
