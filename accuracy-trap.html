The Accuracy Trap

A fraud model scores every case from 0 to 1. Pick a threshold, and every case above it is flagged. Try to find a setting that is both accurate and useful. You will see that those are not the same thing.

Your challenge

Find settings that meet both goals. If they cannot be met, change the fraud rate or the model skill and see why.

    What the scores mean

    • Accuracy is the share of all cases the model got right. When fraud is rare, passing everything scores high.
    • Precision is how many flagged cases were really fraud. Low precision means reviewers drown in false alarms.
    • Recall is how many fraud cases were caught. Low recall means the thing you built the model for is being missed.
    • F1 blends precision and recall into one number, and punishes a model that is weak at either.
